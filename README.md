# Current Target For The Game: 
The current goal for the game is to create a horde survivor game where you have a pistol and you are supposed to defend yourself from the endless amount of zombies that spawn in for a period of time.
The game will start with a small exposition where an npc tells you that the train broke down and it will take x amount of minutes to fix it. 
Then zombies start spawning and the objective is to defend yourself from the waves of zombies that spawn until the train is fixed. That is how we can work a time limit into the narrative of our game.

## Story
- It is night and your train has broken down in the middle of the wilderness
- An old man starts to fix the train and then zombies appear
- The old man tells you it will take 2 minutes to fix the train
- You have to use a pistol to defend yourself from the zombies
- If one you take too many hits you lose
- If you last the 2 minutes, the train is fixed and you can escape

## Minimum Viable Product
- Small Written Dialogue Exposition:
  - “Train Broken Down…”
  - “Will take 2 minutes to fix…”
  - “Cover me while I check the engine…”
  
- Main Game
  - 2 minute timer
  - Fight zombies with a pistol that has a firing cooldown
  - You have a healthbar. If you die it is game over
  - If you last 2 minutes you win

- Key Features
  - Third Person
  - 2 zombie types:
    - One is basic
    - One moves quicker and also randomly jump strafes
  - Simple crosshair and point and click to shoot
  - We will have game objects for zombie spawnpoints
